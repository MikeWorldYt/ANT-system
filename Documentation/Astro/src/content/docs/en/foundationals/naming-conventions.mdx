A nomenclature "**Naming Convention**" is a system for naming things in a specific way. It can be used to create a consistent and organized way to refer to things in a specific context.

## Chill Naming
Uses a two-digit incremental number (01–89), followed by a dot and a name in Train Case (each word capitalized and separated by hyphens).

## Block Naming
Uses increments of 100 (e.g., 100, 200, 300), followed by a dot and a name in UPPERCASE.  
This is a variant of Chill Naming, and its Ideal for top-level or broad categories

## Control Naming
This follows a variable-based structure, where each part is defined by the user's own criteria. Although parts may share patterns or context, the structure is flexible and tailored individually to each case, making it ideal for managing projects and tasks with contextual control and clarity.

### Control Naming (for Folders)
Used by [Workboard Nodes](/foundationals/workboard-nodes), this structure was designed for quick recognition and easy filtering between projects.

That is usually separated by three main Stages joined by underscores:  
> <b>TRACKING</b> _ <b>METADATA</b> _ <b>NAME</b>

## Elements of Naming Conventions

### Identificators (for Workboards)
The identificator is a **code label** that identifies the type of workboard.  
Some of the most common identificators are:
- `PJ`: Project
- `PR`: Practice
- `HW`: Homework
- `CR`: Course
- `TK`: Task

### Tracker (for Workboards)
The tracker is a sequential index that provides chronological order and simple referencing.  
> While many systems can sort by date, a tracker provides a quick way to follow progress without relying on timestamps.  

Used in tandem with the identificator, it instantly communicates the type and order of the workboard.

```fs  title="Use example" ins="PJ.005" ins="PJ.006" ins="HW.015" ins="HW.016"
📁 PJ.005_(24-03-30) Tut . . . ← for projects
📁 PJ.006_(24-04-12) Sal . . .

📁 HW.015_Gramm . . . ← for school homeworks
📁 HW.016_Gramm . . .
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Only need 3-digit counters">
  As a [workboard node](/en/foundationals/workboard-nodes) are temporary and meant to be eventually stored or removed, **Its recommended just use 3-digit counters**,
this allows up to 999 items per type; if exceeded, the counter may will reset.
</Callout>

### Date and Timestamps
Although labeled as “Date”, this element works like a flexible timestamp that adapts to context.  
**When is used must appear within in parentheses**, to ensure quick visual identification.

> This one is not required, but aid to visualize important time such as deadlines, milestones, start dates, periods, seasons, etc.

<h5> You may used these formats: </h5>
- `YY-MM-DD` (Year-Month-Day) — **Most commonly**, used for Start dates, Deadlines, deliveries, etc.  
- `YY-MM` (Year-Month) — For monthly tracking, reports, or retainers.  
- `YY-QQ` (Year-Quarter) — Used for Finantials Quarters (trimesters).  
- `YY-V.1`, `YY-V.2` (Year-Version) — For versioning or iterations.  
- `YY-U1` or `YY-C1` (Year-Unit/Chapter) — For units or chapters in educational contexts.  
- `9G`, `10G`, `12G` (Grade) — For schoolar grades; 9th grade, 10th grade, 12th grade.  
- `S2`, `S3`, `S4` (Semester) — For School Semester, often used in curriculum tracking.

### Breadcrumbs
A breadcrumb defines the contextual type of the workboard, usually based on the parent folder’s category.

> This one is not required but completely recommended, Because acts like a category-tag, aiding as extra filter and avoiding the creation of more levels.

### Holders and Clients
The holder identifies who the workboard belongs (client, brand, organization, school, owner).  
**When is used must appear within in brackets**, to ensure quick visual identification.

> It provides ownership context and is especially useful when managing multiple clients or persons.

```fs  title="Use example" ins="[Oxford]" ins="[Aradir]" ins="[Henry-Dav]"
📁 HW.1 . . . ances [Oxford]_Revolving-deb . . . ← Institutions
📁 PJ.0 . . . eplay [Aradir]_Weather-simul . . . ← Client
📁 PR.0 . . . awing [Henry-Dav]_anatomy-po . . . ← even Yourself (Owner)
```
<Callout type="warning" title="Recomendation">
  Try not use name that are too long or complex.  
  (e.g. "College-of-William-&-Mary-Law-School" instead of "W&M-Laws")
</Callout>

> Besides if you need to keep confidentiality or privacy, You could use a pseudo-name with aid a [Client List](/en/advanced/client-list) document that could well be saved in another site.

```fs title="Use example with a Client List" ins="[C-018-Ara]" ins="[C-031-Bea]"
📁 PJ.0 . . . eplay [C-018-Ara]_Weather-simul . . .  ← Its Aradir, the client n.18
📁 PR.0 . . . eling [C-031-Bea]_Machu-Pichu-o . . .  ← Do you could guess who is?
```

### Name (for Workboards)
This is a short, descriptive label that identifies the content or focus of the workboard,  
In other words, its just the name or title.

Using <u>train-case or kebab-case</u> is recommended to separate any concepts amoung themselves.

```fs title="Use example" ins="landingpage-redesign pet-store" ins="environmental-law-essay" ins="how-to-organize-documents"
📁 PJ.01 . . . ova]_landingpage-redesign pet-store  
📁 HW.02 . . . Ark]_environmental-law-essay 
📁 PJ.03 . . . dYt]_how-to-organize-documents 
```
