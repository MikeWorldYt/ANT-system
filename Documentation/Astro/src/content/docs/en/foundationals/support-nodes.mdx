Support Nodes are folders that are used to store files or directories that are not directly related to the current level. 
Its purpose is to provide a way to store additional information or resources at their same level, In that way support the user.

Each Support Node should has a unique identifier, making it easy to differentiate and quickly identify them.

## 99.Settings Folder
Its should have identifier **99**.  
This [node](/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) (folder) stores preset configurations for programs, the Operating system, or the ANT system itself. It consolidates all environment-specific settings in a single location for immediate access.

Here you should contain subfolders, depending on the type of settings being stored.

<Callout type="warning">
  <p>
    As recommendation, you should only deploy at Level 1 and Level 2 to ensure global accessibility while preventing **file fragmentation**.
  </p>
</Callout>

You can include:
- Program/OS configuration files (e.g., `.env`, `config.json`, etc.)
- System customization assets (icons, themes, scripts)
- Templates or presets
- Automation workflows
- Custom Profiles
- Set-up Programs

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
This node may or not inherit their name from parent node.  
Just looking like **99.Settings** is enough.

<h5> Uttility </h5> 
Acts as a central hub for storing configurations, presets, and other settings that are required for your programs and files.

## 98.Review Folder
Its should have identifier **98**.  
This [node](/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) (folder) contains all your files and directories that need review;
- Need renaming
- Have no classification
- or Even are not from the current level.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
This node will inherit just their name from parent node, with prefix **98.** before the name.  

```ansi {5}
[32m 📁[37m 04.Marketing                      [35m ←−−− Parent
[32m  ├── 📁[37m 01.Foundations of Marketing
[32m  ├── 📁[37m 02.Web Analytics and SEO
[32m  ├── 📁[37m . . .
[32m  ├── 📁[37m 98.Marketing                 [35m ←−−− Review Folder
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m Online marketing Paolini course
[32m  │    ├── 📄[37m Build-a-Successful-Social-Media-Strategy.pdf
[32m  │    ├── [37m🖼 bf25a6e4ab1909bfcb25c50e3368a8.jpg
[32m  │    └── [37m🖼 . . .
```

<h5> Uttility </h5> 
✅ Its functionality is to be like a Sandbox; This helps the user and the system alike in avoiding mess and maintaining organizational integrity.  
✅ By routing unclear or unclassified files to a properly marked staging area, it is become a temporary place for these without compromising rest of files.

## 97.Templates Folder
Its should have identifier **97**.  
This [node](/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) (folder) stores blueprint structures for [workboard nodes](#workboard-folders). These templates provide standardized starting points for [lifespan nodes](/foundationals/core-concepts#lifespan) (folders) that frequently created.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
Does not inherit parent name. Always appears as **97.Templates** with relevant subfolders:

```ansi {2}
[32m 📁[37m 02.Video-Edition                      [35m ←−−− Parent
[32m  ├── 📁[37m 97.Templates                     [35m ←−−− Templates Folder
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m PJ.999_(YY-MM-DD) Breadcrumb [Holder]_unnamed-project
[32m  │    └── 📁[37m PR.999_(YY-MM-DD) Breadcrumb [Holder]_new-practice
[32m  ├── 📁[37m PJ.133_(25-07-03) Video-Edition [Mr-Beast]_template-use-example
[32m  ├── 📁[37m . . .
```

<h5> Utility </h5>
Serves as a **rapid deployment hub** for [workboards nodes](#workboard-folders) by:  
✅ Providing one-click copy/paste templates for new [workboards nodes](#workboard-folders) that just require renaming  
✅ Maintaining consistent structures for temporary projects  
✅ It may hold starter files that can be used for new projects

## 90.Resources Folder
Its should have identifier **90**.  
This [node](/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) (folder) serves as a centralized repository for frequently accessed reference materials, reusable assets, and static support files. It houses essential resources that remain consistently valuable across multiple projects or operations.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
This node appears as `90.Resources-of-` and continue inherit their name from parent node.

```ansi {2}
[32m 📁[37m 02.Video-Edition                     [35m  ←−−− Parent
[32m  ├── 📁[37m 90.Resources-of-Video-Edition   [35m  ←−−− Resources Folder
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m 01.Templates-and-presets
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m 02.Music-and-SFX
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m . . .
```

<h5> Utility </h5>
Functions as an **always-available toolkit** for your projects, tasks or homework. It provides a place to store frequently used files, templates, and resources that are accessible from current level.

## (00) Done Folder
Its should have identifier **00**.  
This [node](/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) serves exclusively as the **final archive** for completed outputs. When a [workboard folder](#workboard-folders) reaches end-of-life after project/task completion, only the finalized assets are preserved here.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
Always it should appears as **00.Done** at every level where workboards exist.  
if that you want, you may name it whatever you want but need to use the prefix **00.** before the name.

```ansi {2}
[32m 📁[37m 02.Video-Edition                      [35m ←−−− Parent
[32m  ├── 📁[37m 00.Edition-Portfolio             [35m ←−−− Done Folder
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m 01.Templates-and-presets
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m 02.Music-and-SFX
[32m  │    ├── 📁[37m . . .
```

## Temporal Folders (~)
Alright, thats not a identifier as such, it's just a mark.  
This special marker (not a numbered identifier) designates **ultra-short-lived storage** for active work files. Unlike the structured [98.Review Folder](#98review-folder), `~` nodes are disposable containers for assets during active manipulation, serving as temporary holding areas that should be deleted immediately after use.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
Always prefixed with `~` and continue with any name you want.
