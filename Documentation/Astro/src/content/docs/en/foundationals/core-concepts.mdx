**ANT has a hierarchical structure** adapted to be immediately implemented in the conventional tree structure.  
However, **due to it's has a decentralized modular infrastructure**, it follows a specific structural rules that ensures its proper functioning.  
To understand this, we must first grasp the terms [Levels](#levels), [Nodes](#nodes) and [Modules](#modules) within this system;

## Levels
The term "**Levels**" refers to the depth of the folder in relation to her root folder

Although we advise keeping 3 Levels, there may be more. <br/> However, it is recommended not to have too many levels and to use them only in certain cases.
Because it adds complexity to the system and can make it difficult to navigate.

## Nodes
The term "**Node**" refers to each folder.

<div class="pic-container not-content">
    <img src="https://i.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExd2phNHR3aGlkZ281NDh6NHhmbDM2OThvc2MxZ3R6MDN2NmVwcTA0ZCZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9dg/OPUFqtt4ydWj5rmPNM/giphy.gif" />

This system is structured in a way that each folder its conected and follow thier way/path. <br/>
Each node or *"folder"* have one or more in/out ways, Its being part of a [module](#modules), and in some cases having a lifespan (existence).

## Modules
The term "**Module**" refers to a grouping of [nodes](#nodes) in relation to their level, It is a **category**.
<div class="pic-container not-content">
    <img src="https://i.pinimg.com/originals/c5/fc/93/c5fc9347527030b2fb328370729500d4.jpg" />
</div>

It means that, regardless of their connections, nodes should maintain a hierarchical grouping and these itself consistent within the conventional [Tree data structure](/reference/another-concepts#tree-data-structure).<br/>
This keeping a logical, consistent and easy to navigate, tho doesn't take away their flexibility.

## Lifespan
Some nodes may have a lifespan, such as a [workboard](/en/foundationals/workboard-nodes) or a [temporal folder](/en/foundationals/support-nodes#temporal-folders).  
That means that these nodes are useful in a period of time and should be deleted after use.

Lifespan node Cycle: 

<Steps>
    1. **Creation:**  
    Some nodes are created from templates presets for quickly join to storage.  
    This ones must be immediately identifiable and their content inside does not always have to have a strict order.


    2. **Active Use:**  
    Commonly its used as a repository with a self-contained role, Although is constantly followed by the system itself aid with trackers or special marks.

    3. **Delivery:**  
    When the goal is done (e.g. finished the project), their content can become useless.

    4. **Review and Archive:**  
    Here the content is valued to know if it may be reused in the future:  
    ● If the content is useful, it can be archived there or move another places.  
    ● Else the content is deleted.
    
    5. **Death:**  
    Once it is considered obsolete, the node is removed from the system.
</Steps>
