## Level 1
The first level lies directly inside the storage root.  
This means that we are talking about the **principal categories** that define the content of each [node](/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes).

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
In this level, you must use a [Sequential Chill Naming](/en/foundationals/naming-conventions#sequential-chill-naming) Convention.

<LinkCard
  title="Sequential Chill Naming"
  href="/en/foundationals/naming-conventions#sequential-chill-naming"
  description="Uses increments of 100 (e.g., 100, 200, 300), followed by a dot and a name in UPPERCASE (all laters are capitalized). Ideal for top-level or broad categories"
/>

```fs {2-4}
 📁 Root
  ├── 📁 100.PERSONAL
  ├── 📁 200.WORK
  ├── 📁 300.PROJECTS
```

<Callout type="danger" title="Only be named once">
  This is defined the first time and should not be renamed again to prevent any conflicts with programs with linked paths, dependencies or shortcuts.
</Callout>

<h5> Key points: </h5>
- **Storage** This level must not contain files, external folders, or unorganized content. It should only include [nodes (folders)](/en/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) considered as main categories defining their own content.
- **Support Nodes:** These reserve the sequence 900 combined with their identifier (e.g. 998.Review
instead of 98.Review).

##### Principal Categories Recomendation:

```fs {2-9}
 📁 Root
  ├── 📁 100.PERSONAL
  ├── 📁 200.WORK
  ├── 📁 300.PROJECTS
  ├── 📁 400.ACADEMIC
  ├── 📁 500.HOBBIES
  ├── 📁 900.Resources
  ├── 📁 998.Review (Index)
  └── 📁 999.Settings
```

## Level 2
The second level is a child of a [Level 1](#level-1) node, these must use subcategories of the parent class.  
This means that we are talking about the areas, subjects, types of job, or another group related their parent node.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
In this level, you must use a [Chill Naming](/en/foundationals/naming-conventions#chill-naming) Convention.

<LinkCard
  title="Chill Naming"
  href="/en/foundationals/naming-conventions#chill-naming"
  description="Uses a two-digit incremental number (01–89), followed by a dot and <b>their name</b> in Train Case (each word capitalized and separated by hyphens)."
/>

```fs {3-4, 7}
 📁 Root
  ├── 📁 100.PERSONAL
  │    ├── 📁 01.Documents
  │    ├── 📁 02.Gallery
  │    └── 📁 . . .
  ├── 📁 200.WORK
  │    ├── 📁 01.Development
  │    └── 📁 . . .
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep names without duplicates or redundant">
  Avoid duplicate names or semantically related names that just could be separated further in the next level.
</Callout>

<h5> Key points: </h5>
- **Storage** This level must not contain files, external folders, or unorganized content. It should only include [nodes (folders)](/en/foundationals/core-concepts#nodes) considered as subcategories of the parent defining their own content.
- **Support Nodes:** These have reserve sequences "90-99" and "00" for their identifiers.

## Level 3
The third level is a child of a [Level 2](#level-2) node, **supports additional organization**, either through category extensions or [**Workboard Nodes**](/en/foundationals/workboard-nodes) **(folders)**.

<h5> Naming Convention: </h5>
You can use 2 naming conventions;  
**1.-** If you need an extend *with subjects, topics, more categories,* use the `Chill Naming` yet.  

<LinkCard
  title="Chill Naming"
  href="/en/foundationals/naming-conventions#chill-naming"
  description="Uses a two-digit incremental number (01–89), followed by a dot and <b>their name</b> in Train Case (each word capitalized and separated by hyphens)."
/>

```fs {4-6}
 📁 Root    
  ├── 📁 100.PERSONAL
  │    ├── 📁 01.Documents
  │    │    ├── 📁 01.Personal
  │    │    ├── 📁 02.Finantials
  │    │    ├── 📁 03.Legals
  │    │    └── 📁 . . .
  │    ├── 📁 02.Gallery
  │    └── 📁 . . .
```

**2.-** If the you need manage it *for tasks, projects, etc.** use the `Control Naming` Convention.  

<LinkCard
  title="Control Naming"
  href="/en/foundationals/naming-conventions#control-naming"
  description="This follows a variable-based structure, where each part is defined by the user's own criteria. Although parts may share patterns or context, the structure is flexible and tailored individually to each case, making it ideal for managing projects and tasks with contextual control and clarity."
/>

```fs {5, 8-9}
 📁 Root    
  ├── 📁 100.PERSONAL
  ├── 📁 200.WORK
  │    ├── 📁 01.Development
  │    │    ├── 📁 PJ.006_(23-03-03) Landingpage [Wallter-White]_Astro Carwash 
  │    │    └── 📁 . . .
  │    ├── 📁 02.Video-Edition
  │    │    ├── 📁 PJ.130_(25-07-03) Tutorial [MikeWorldYt]_Organize Documents
  │    │    ├── 📁 PJ.131_(25-07-03) Review [Isa-Marcial]_Huawei pura 70 
  │    │    └── 📁 . . .
```
