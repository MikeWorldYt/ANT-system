On open, the app compares its version with the one published on GitHub, unless you turned off **Check for updates automatically** in About. If there is a newer one, choose **Update**, **Remind me later** or **Skip version**. **Check for updates** in About runs the check on demand, even for a skipped version.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Updates" />

- **Windows:** Update closes the app, swaps the executable and needs `FM_Updater.bat` in `999.Settings/00.Core` of your storage.
- **Linux and macOS:** the in-app update is not available. Download the new archive from the [Downloads page](/en/start-here/download) and replace the old file.
