## Views

Use the view button in the toolbar, or right-click the background, to switch between six views of the same storage: List, Details, Cards, Grid, Columns and Graph.

The breadcrumb shows the open folder. Double-click it, or press `Ctrl+L`, to type a path. The separators between its names list the sibling folders. The back, forward and up buttons walk your history. The side panel on the right shows the details and a preview of the selected item; collapse it with its button or `Ctrl+Right`.

### List

The contents of one folder as plain rows. Use it for a compact, quiet listing. Double-click a folder, or press `Enter`, to go into it.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: List view" />

### Details

Like List, with columns beside each name: **Date created**, **Date modified**, **Type** and **Size**. Use it when you need to compare dates or sizes.

- **Sort** by clicking a column header; click it again to flip the direction. **Type** and **Owner** do not sort.
- **Resize** a column by dragging the right edge of its header. Double-click the edge to fit the column to its contents.
- **Right-click** the header to pick **Size column to fit**, **Size all columns to fit**, or to show and hide columns. **Status** and **Owner** are hidden by default; **Name** always stays.
- **Folder sizes** are summed in the background, so they appear a moment after the list opens.

Column widths are remembered per folder.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Details view" />

### Cards

The contents of one folder as icons with the kind of item under each name. Use it for a visual overview that still tells folders from files.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Cards view" />

### Grid

The contents of one folder as large icons, with thumbnails for files that have one. Use it for browsing images and documents. Arrow keys move across rows and columns.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Grid view" />

### Columns

One column per level, with the selection staying at the right as you go deeper. Each column has its own sort, see [Sorting](#sorting). Use it to keep the parent levels in sight while you browse.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Columns view" />

### Graph

A mind map of the NODE and WKB folders that unfolds to the right from the storage root, joined with bracket lines. Use it to read the structure of a storage at a glance.

- **Click a card** to select it and unfold it. Clicking an open card that is not selected only moves the selection to it; clicking the selected card folds it.
- **Support folders** (`00.*`, `REC`, `SS`, `RES`, `REV`) stack above or below their node. Opening one shows its contents in place of the sub-nodes, which blur behind. Only one support folder is open at a time.
- **Worknodes and plain folders** unfold into a card per subfolder plus one box with their files.
- **The path** from the root to the selection glows and shifts slightly right, and the lines of the selected branch are tinted.
- **Zoom** with `Ctrl` + mouse wheel, or `Ctrl` + `+` / `Ctrl` + `−`. Hold `Space` and drag to pan.

The graph remembers which folders you left open, per storage. Opening a split from the Graph uses the Default View.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Graph view" />

### Default view

In **Settings → General → Default View** choose the view that new windows and tabs start with: Last used, List, Details, Cards, Grid, Columns or Graph. With **On launch: Continue where I left off**, the app reopens the last view you used.

## Navigation pane

The pane on the left of the views charts where things are. It never lists files; they stay in the views.

- **Quick access** heads the pane: **FAVORITES**, **DOWNLOADS** and **REVIEW**, each of which can be turned off in **Settings → General**.
- **The storage folders** follow, as an indented tree. Click the caret to unfold or fold a folder without opening it.
- **The native folders** (Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos and Desktop) close the pane when **Show all native System folders** is on.

A click only marks a row. Double-click it, or press `Enter`, to open it in the views. A slow second click on a name renames it, as does `F2`. Move with the arrow keys: right unfolds, left folds or goes to the parent.

Press `Ctrl+Left` to show or hide the pane. Drag its right edge, or focus the edge and press the left and right arrows, to resize it. Dragging it too narrow closes it. The width is remembered.

Right-click a folder for **Open**, **Open folder location**, **Open in new tab**, **Open in split tab**, **Copy path** and **Hide element**. The pane only moves around, so the menu has no actions that change a folder.

### Switching storage

The footer shows the name of the open storage. Click it, or press `Up` or `Down` on it, to open a menu with every storage you know. Pick one to switch to it. A storage that moved is marked **Not found**. **Manage storages…** opens the storage picker. The gear beside it opens **Settings**.

## Preview

Select one file and the side panel shows its preview below the details. These types are supported:

- **Images**: PNG, JPG, JFIF, GIF, WebP, BMP, SVG and AVIF.
- **PDF**: one page at a time, turned with the buttons below it or the arrow keys.
- **Video**: MP4, M4V, WebM, MOV and OGV. It plays on its own and starts muted. If Windows lacks the video's codec, a still frame shows instead.
- **Audio**: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, OGA and FLAC. It plays on its own and starts muted.

Once you turn the sound on, the next previews keep it on for five minutes.

## Sorting

The sort button in the toolbar sorts the open list by **Track**, **Name**, **Status**, **Date created**, **Date modified** or **Size**, ascending or descending. Every list keeps its own sort; in the Columns view, every column does.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Screenshot: Sorting" />

Whatever the sort, the folders numbered 90 to 99, plus 998 and 999, always stay at the end of their list. That keeps the [support nodes](/en/foundationals/support-nodes) in a fixed place.
